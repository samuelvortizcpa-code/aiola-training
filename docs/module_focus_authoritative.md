# Days 31-90 Module Focus Statements (Authoritative)

This file is the in-repo mirror of **Aiola_CPA_-_Tax_Advisory_Associate_90-Day_Onboarding_Plan.pdf** (the signed plan). It is the authoritative source for any Days 31-90 build. If any other document (Jesse_Onboarding_Snapshot.docx, Jesse_Onboarding_Outline_v4.docx, or any planning doc) conflicts with these focus statements, the text below wins.

---

## Module 1 — Days 31-40 — Asset Protection & Entity Structuring

Focus: The Aiola CPA standard recommendation (Wyoming holding LLC + sub-LLCs + operations LLC) and when to deviate. Disregarded entity vs. partnership at the holding level. Weigh asset protection, anonymity, and tax impact. Deed transfer mechanics and due-on-sale clauses. Umbrella insurance as substitute or complement. S Corp/C Corp interaction (never for rentals).

End-of-module deliverable: Presentation to management on recommended asset protection/entity structure, pros and cons, balancing technical and practical advice, and state considerations.

## Module 2 — Days 41-50 — Accounting & Banking System

Focus: QBO, chart of accounts, classes/locations. Bank and credit card account strategies. How to review books and read financials. What advisory handles vs. what passes to tax prep and accounting.

End-of-module deliverable: YTD review of books for a real client - draft a memo of action items and corrections for review.

## Module 3 — Days 51-60 — STR Tax Strategy Deep Dive

Focus: Study the STR tax strategy (highly technical) - commit the qualifications to memory. Understand the black and white rules, and the gray areas (and our position on them). Read and cite regulations, tax court cases, and other authoritative guidance. Understand the details and ask questions. You need to be an expert on this.

End-of-module deliverable: Create and deliver to management a tax savings analysis report for five separate clients who are targeting an STR. Quantify the real amount of tax savings and benefit of the strategy in each case.

## Module 4 — Days 61-70 — REPS Deep Dive

Focus: Study REPS (highly technical) - commit the qualifications to memory. Understand the black and white rules, and the gray areas (and our position on them). Read and cite regulations, tax court cases, and other authoritative guidance. Understand the details and ask questions. You need to be an expert on this.

End-of-module deliverable: REPS qualification memo for a real client, which includes a blueprint on how the client can qualify using multiple different scenarios. Analyze criteria and factor in aggregation elections.

## Module 5 — Days 71-80 — Time Log Mastery

Focus: Master the art of the time log. Learn which hours count for material participation and which don't. Understand how to build a solid and defensible log with appropriate substantiation.

End-of-module deliverable: Completed TLR submitted to management for review, and revamp the time log knowledgebase with intense research and summarization.

## Module 6 — Days 81-90 — Client Ownership

Focus: One to two weeks running a real new client engagement under manager supervision. Lead and own the client relationship from the beginning. Meet with the client to set expectations and align on missing items, prep for the ISM, lead the live ISM with manager shadowing, complete and deliver all post-ISM tasks to manager.

End-of-module deliverable: Lead live onboarding meeting, lead live ISM, complete TSR.
